Health tracking, reimagined.

  • AI report analysis

    Upload any medical PDF: blood work, scans, prescriptions. Healio extracts the key markers and explains them.

  • Body system tracking

    Cardiovascular, respiratory, metabolic, skeletal, neurological and hormonal. See which systems need attention.

  • Family health groups

    Add family members, manage their reports and see everyone in one place. Care for everyone, together.

  • Smart goals and challenges

    Steps, hydration and sleep targets that adjust to your progress.

  • Trends over time

    See how your markers move across reports over months and years, and catch changes early.

  • Private by design

    Your health data is yours. It’s never sold, and row-level security keeps each account’s data separate.

Meet Healio, your health AI.

Healio reads your uploaded reports, understands your history and explains what it means in plain language. It suggests next steps and flags anything worth raising with your doctor.
