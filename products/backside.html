Idea: flip back with the same hotkey
Point anywhere. Start typing.

A second surface, one hotkey away.

  • Point, and just type

    Move your mouse anywhere on the canvas and start typing. A note appears right at your cursor, no click needed.

  • Every block you need

    Text, checklists, sticky notes, code, tables, images, voice and video notes. Right-click anywhere to add one.

  • Brainstorm visually

    Rectangles, diamonds, speech and thought bubbles, arrows. Sketch connections, not just text.

  • Truly infinite

    Pan in any direction with no edge. A live mini map shows every block you’ve placed, and a click jumps you there.

  • Instant search

    ⌘F finds any note on the canvas, however far you’ve panned.

  • Keyboard first

    Nudge blocks with arrow keys, duplicate with ⌘⇧D, undo with ⌘Z. Press ? for the cheatsheet.

  • Always there, never in the way

    No Dock icon and no windows. It starts at login, waits until you need it and updates itself.

Every Mac has a hundred spare pixels of thinking space hiding behind the desktop. Backside just gives you a way in.
