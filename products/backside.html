How It Works

From desktop to
canvas, instantly.

01
Install & Grant Access

Drag Backside to Applications. A short walkthrough asks for the one permission it needs — Accessibility — so the global hotkey works from anywhere.

02
Flip With a Hotkey

Press Shift + ↑ from any app. Your screen peels away to reveal your canvas — a plain, private surface, not a screenshot of your desktop.

03
A Thirty-Second Tour

The first time your canvas opens, a short interactive walkthrough shows you point-and-type, the right-click menu, and the mini map — then gets out of your way for good.

04
Capture, Then Flip Back

Type, sketch, drop in images. Press Shift + ↓ and you're back to exactly what you were doing — your canvas waiting untouched for next time.

Questions

Before you
flip your screen.

Does Backside see my screen or record it?

No. The flip is a plain generated sheet, not a screenshot — Backside never captures or reads your desktop. The only permission it asks for is Accessibility, so the global hotkey (Shift + ↑/↓) works from any app.

Where is my canvas stored?

Locally on your Mac first, so it's instant even offline. Backside syncs changes in the background so your notes are backed up without you thinking about it.

Is it really free?

Yes — Backside is free to download and use. It updates itself in the background, so you're never stuck reinstalling to get the latest fixes and features.

Will it slow down my Mac?

It's built to sit idle until you need it — no Dock icon, no menu bar clutter, negligible CPU use in the background. The canvas only renders what's actually on screen, so it stays fast even with hundreds of blocks.
