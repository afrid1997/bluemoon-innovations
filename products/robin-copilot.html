Two we’ve built.

BeBe

BeBe is the companion inside the Being Better app, and now inside WhatsApp too. It’s one BeBe in both places, with one personality and one memory, so a conversation started in the app carries on in a chat thread.

It’s built on a Gemini-powered chat function, remembers the facts you share and is instructed never to diagnose or wave away a symptom.

Robin Copilot

Robin is a lightweight chat widget you add to a web app with one script tag. It answers from your knowledge base and can take the wheel: opening the right page and highlighting the button a user is looking for.

We built it for a Frappe-based system, with a dashboard for your team, WhatsApp mirroring and approval steps for sensitive actions.

Message in, action out.

The same five steps, whichever channel your team lives in.

  1. A message arrives

    Someone writes on WhatsApp, Slack, Teams or your website.

  2. The agent understands and answers

    It reads the context, checks your systems and replies, or says it doesn’t know.

  3. An approval gate, when it matters

    Refunds, discounts and other consequential actions wait for a person.

  4. A human reviews

    The right person sees the request, the context and the agent’s suggestion.

  5. The action runs

    The agent finishes the job and tells everyone what it did.
