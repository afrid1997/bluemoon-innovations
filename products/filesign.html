Free for everyone. Built in India, under the IT Act, 2000.

Built for the way people actually sign.

  • Free to send and sign

    No subscription and no trial that ends. Create an account, upload a PDF and send it.

  • Signers need no account

    Invite anyone by email. They get a unique link, confirm with a one-time code and sign in the browser.

  • Four ways to sign

    Draw with a trackpad or finger, wave your index finger at the webcam, use your phone as a touchpad, or photograph a paper signature.

  • Grounded in Indian law

    Signatures follow the IT Act, 2000 and the DPDP Act, 2023. Each signer gives explicit consent, logged against the document.

  • A full audit trail

    Timestamp, IP address and a SHA-256 hash of the file are recorded for every signature. Download the finished PDF any time.

  • Teams, templates and signing order

    Workspaces with owner, admin, member and viewer roles. Save NDAs and contracts as templates. Choose who is notified, and when.
