Scalp Sentinel BB01 is in prototype.

Four sensors in a comb.

  • Macro-sight follicle tracking

    A 3MP camera with 60× magnification captures follicle-level images every session, so you can track density and health over time.

  • A 3D scalp blueprint

    A 6-axis motion sensor follows every stroke. The data is stitched into a map of your scalp, zone by zone.

  • Sebum and oiliness

    An RGBC colour sensor measures surface gloss across the scalp, showing where it runs oily or dry.

  • A hydration proxy

    Humidity sensing near the scalp estimates how well your moisture barrier is holding up between sessions.
