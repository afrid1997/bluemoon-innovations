Six steps, no black boxes.

You’re in the room for every decision, and every trade-off gets explained.

Read the full process

  1. Understand the problem

    We go deep on where it started, what keeps feeding it, and the workarounds you already tried.

  2. Explore your ideas

    Mockups, sketches, a voice note, a chit of paper. We treat all of it as a clue to what success looks like.

  3. Debate and refine

    The team argues honestly: is this the right fix? We come back with a recommendation, not a quote.

  4. Agree on the approach

    One shared scope and one set of milestones. No ambiguity left.

  5. Build the system

    Architecture and business logic first, then the interface, then the AI on top.

  6. Adopt and grow

    We train your people, watch adoption and send you the numbers after launch.
